Most RAG interview prep is definitions. Interviews are not.

Nobody asks "what is a vector database" any more. They ask:
"Your RAG app is giving wrong answers. Is it retrieval or generation, and how do you prove it?"

That is the shift we see with every batch of engineers going through production RAG on Google Cloud. The questions are scenarios, and the answer has to be something you would actually do on Monday morning.

So we put together the Top 10 Practical RAG Interview Questions, ordered easy → tough, each with an answer you can defend ⤵️

🟢 Easy
1. The minimum RAG pipeline for a folder of policy PDFs
2. Picking a chunk size, and what breaks when it is wrong

🟡 Average
3. Wrong answers: retrieval problem or generation problem?
4. Vector search keeps missing product codes and error IDs
5. The model answers confidently when the documents don't
6. Keeping the index in sync with daily updates and deletes
7. Eight seconds and too expensive per answer: what to fix first

🔴 Tough
8. Evaluating a change before shipping, without a labelling budget
9. One RAG service, many customers: tenant A must never see tenant B
10. A retrieved document says "ignore previous instructions"

Swipe through. Every page has the question, the answer, and the reasoning an interviewer is listening for.

If you can walk through all 10 without notes, you are ready for most GenAI engineer interviews in 2026.
