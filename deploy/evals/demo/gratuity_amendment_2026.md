# Payment of Gratuity (Amendment) Notification 2026

Synthetic document for the ingestion rehearsal. Not a real amendment.

effective_from: 2026-10-01

## Amendment of section 4

In section 4(1) of the Payment of Gratuity Act, 1972, for the words "five years", the words "six years" shall be
substituted. Gratuity shall accordingly be payable to an employee on the termination of employment after rendering
continuous service for not less than six years.

## Savings

Nothing in this notification affects gratuity that became payable before the date on which it takes effect.
