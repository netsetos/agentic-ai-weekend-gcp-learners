# Smoke note

Synthetic document for the reindex smoke (deploy/smoke/smoke_reindex.py). Every fact is invented.

## SM-01 - The smoke lantern

The smoke lantern is kept in bay 4 of the Pune warehouse. It is checked on the first Monday of every month
and the check is initialled in the bay register.

## SM-02 - The ladder

The extension ladder is stored against the east wall of bay 2 and must be signed out in the register before
it leaves the warehouse floor.
